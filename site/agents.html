What every kit contains

Whichever agent you design, all of this is generated from your answers.

Its instructionsA system prompt written for your business: the job in your words, what it handles, how it hands work to a person, and your "never do" rules as hard rules.
Its toolsEvery action it may take, in OpenAI, Anthropic and MCP formats, each marked read, internal or external, for the systems you actually use.
A workflow per capabilityWhat starts it, the steps in order, the cadence where there is one, and which steps wait for approval. Usable as a spec for n8n, Make or Zapier.
The autonomy dialWatch, Draft, Approve, Operate, as a policy you enforce in code. The agent starts at Draft: it prepares, a person sends. You raise it when you've seen enough.
Its recordsJSON Schemas for everything it keeps, always including the approval queue and an append-only audit log of what it did and why.
GuardrailsNo invented prices or promises, permanent opt-outs, quiet hours, and instructions inside a customer's message treated as information, never as orders.
A setup checklistThe facts only you can supply, like working hours, real routes and review links, listed setting by setting. The kit never guesses them.
Acceptance testsOne per capability: the line that says whether it works, to run before it goes near a customer.

What an agent can be made of

Any of these can go into any kit. Each one arrives as a playbook, a workflow and a test, and the test is shown here because it is the line that says whether it works. Adding capabilities never adds a price per item: the kit is priced by the size of the agent, and the configurator says before you click if something would take it from one job to a whole function.

Why it's a catalog and not a blank page

Because a blank page is how software projects die. "Tell us what you want and we'll estimate it" produces a number nobody can defend, a scope that grows in the dark, and an invoice that arrives with a surprise in it.

Each role above is a shape that works: a set of capabilities that do one job together, each with a written acceptance test. The bespoke part (your systems, your channels, your tone, your rules about what it must never do without asking) is what your kit is generated from, and it is what makes the agent yours.

If what you need is not quite any of these, pick the closest. Every file in a kit is plain JSON or Markdown, written to be edited.
