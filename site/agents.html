Why it's a catalog and not a blank page

Because a blank page is how software projects die. "Tell us what you want and we'll estimate it" produces a number nobody can defend, a scope that grows in the dark, and an invoice that arrives with a surprise in it.

Each role above has been built before, which is precisely why it can carry a fixed price and a fixed date. The bespoke part — your prices, your systems, your tone, your rules about what it must never do without asking — is the configuration, and it's what makes the agent yours. The machinery underneath is shared, hardened, and monitored, and that is entirely to your benefit: your agent inherits every fix ever made for anyone else's.

If what you need is genuinely outside all of this, that's a Custom Build from $2,500, scoped and priced in writing before anything begins.
