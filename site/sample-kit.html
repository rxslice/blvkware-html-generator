Read it in this order

  1. README.md: what the kit is, what it is not, and how to load it into OpenAI, Anthropic, an MCP client, n8n, Make or Zapier.

  3. policies/autonomy.json: what it may do on its own at each level. It starts at Draft.
  4. workflows/quote.followup.json: the job itself, step by step, with the steps that wait for a person.
  5. tests/acceptance.md: what it has to pass before it touches a real customer.

What changes in yours

  • The job, in your words. What you type about the work is what the agent is told, word for word.
  • Your never list. Every line becomes a hard rule in its instructions and its guardrails.
  • Your systems and channels. An integration note for each system it operates, and only the tools those systems need.
  • What it can do. One workflow, one set of instructions and one acceptance test per capability you choose, from {{N_CAPS}}.
  • How much it may do alone. The autonomy level you are working towards, enforced tool by tool.
