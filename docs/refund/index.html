Refund Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

If something you bought from BlvkWare isn't right for you, you can have your money back within 30 days. You don't need to give a reason.

Shared HALLUX and CORDON credit packs

Credit packs bought through PayPal can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase, for any reason. Write to russ@blvkware.dev from the email address you paid with, or with your order ID, and the refund goes back through PayPal. The credit on the key is removed when it is refunded.

Previous subscription payments

Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for previous subscriptions purchased through Paddle. Paddle handles billing, invoices and subscription refunds. BlvkWare provides technical support. Previous agent-kit purchases were processed by Gumroad; prepaid API credits are purchased separately through PayPal.

30-day refund. You can ask for a full refund of any subscription payment, the first one or a renewal, within 30 days of being charged, for any reason.

How to ask: go to paddle.net and look up your order with the email you paid with, or use the link in your Paddle receipt. You can also write to russ@blvkware.dev and we will arrange it with Paddle. Paddle sends the money back to the payment method you used.

When a payment is refunded, the API key it paid for stops working. Refunds are also governed by Paddle's refund policy and buyer terms, which may give you more rights than this page, never fewer.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from the link in your Paddle receipt or at paddle.net. You won't be charged again, and your key keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling on its own doesn't refund the current period; ask for a refund as above if you want one.

Previous agent-kit purchases

Previous kit purchases were processed by Gumroad. You can ask for a full refund within 30 days of purchase, for any reason, from your Gumroad receipt or by writing to russ@blvkware.dev. A refunded kit's licence key stops downloading.

Per-call payments

A per-call payment over x402 is taken only when the call is answered. If a paid call failed or returned the wrong result, write to russ@blvkware.dev with the transaction ID and it will be refunded to the paying wallet.

Your statutory rights

Nothing on this page reduces any right you have under the consumer law where you live.
