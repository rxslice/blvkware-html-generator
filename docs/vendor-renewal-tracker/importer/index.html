What this can and cannot do

It can spot a merchant charging you on a regular cadence, strip the payment-processor noise out of the descriptor, work out monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or annual from the gaps, and total up what it implies you are spending a year.

It cannot see anything that is not in the file. Charges on a personal card, fees deducted from revenue before it reaches your bank, invoices paid by transfer that look like any other payment, and app-store subscriptions billed through a platform are all invisible here. Pull every source you have.

It guesses. The category comes from keywords in the merchant name and the cycle comes from the spacing of the charges. Two charges 31 days apart might be a monthly subscription or a coincidence. That is why every field is editable, and why the confidence column exists.
