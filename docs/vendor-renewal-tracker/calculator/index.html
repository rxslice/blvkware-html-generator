    How the deadline maths works

    Three dates matter and most people only track one.

    Renewal dateWhen the new term begins and you are charged
    Notice deadlineRenewal date minus the notice period. Your actual deadline
    Act-by dateNotice deadline minus a week, because vendors are slow to reply

    Two things catch people out. First, some contracts count the notice period from the anniversary of signature rather than the end of the term, which moves the deadline earlier. Second, some require notice by post or through a support portal, so an email to your account manager is not notice at all. Read the Notices clause before you rely on any date on this page.
