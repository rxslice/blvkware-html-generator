Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026

BlvkWare does not run analytics, does not set cookies, and has no user accounts. The tools on this site run entirely inside your browser and send nothing to any BlvkWare server — because there isn't one. This page explains precisely what does happen, including the parts involving third parties.

Who is responsible

BlvkWare is a sole operation run by William Russell Wheeler, based in Mississippi, United States, and serving clients remotely nationwide. For anything on this page, write to russ@blvkware.dev.

What this website collects

No accounts. No cookies. No analytics. No advertising or tracking pixels. There is no sign-up anywhere on this site and no profile is ever built about you.

Hosting logs

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records standard request data — your IP address, browser user agent, and which files were requested — for delivery and abuse prevention. BlvkWare cannot see these logs. GitHub's handling of that data falls under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Your browser contacts Google to fetch them, which means Google receives your IP address and user agent as part of that request. No other Google service is used, and no font request carries any information about what you are doing on the site.

What the tools do with your input

AUGUR, SCRY and SIGIL are client-side applications. Their code runs on your device. There is no BlvkWare backend receiving your prompts, your API key, or your results.

Your AI provider key

The tools need an AI model to work. On this public site you supply your own key from a provider you choose. That key is stored in your browser's localStorage, on your own device, and is sent directly from your browser to the provider you selected — Google AI Studio, OpenRouter, or Groq — over HTTPS. It never passes through BlvkWare. Clearing your browser storage, or using the "Forget key" button, removes it permanently.

Whatever you type into a tool is sent to that provider along with the request, and is handled under their privacy terms. Read those if you intend to enter anything sensitive.

Data kept in your browser

The tools store a small amount of state in localStorage on your device: your chosen provider and API key, your light/dark theme preference, whether you've dismissed the support dialog, and — in SIGIL — the builds you've generated, so they survive a page reload. None of it leaves your device. Clearing site data in your browser deletes all of it.

AUGUR and third-party page readers

AUGUR analyses a public web page you nominate. A browser cannot fetch an arbitrary website directly, so the address you enter is passed to public read-only relay services — currently corsproxy.io, api.allorigins.win and r.jina.ai — which retrieve the page and return it. Those services see the URL you asked for and your IP address. They are independent third parties operating under their own terms. Only the address is sent; no other information about you is included.

SCRY's "Email me this map"

This does not transmit anything on its own. It opens your own email application with the map already written into a draft addressed to BlvkWare. Nothing is sent unless you choose to press send, at which point it becomes an ordinary email — see below.

When you email BlvkWare

Email sent to russ@blvkware.dev is received and stored in Zoho Mail. Your address and the content of your message are kept so the conversation can continue and so records of any work can be maintained. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never added to a marketing list without you asking. Ask and it will be deleted.

Clients and paid work

If you engage BlvkWare for a paid service, the information needed to deliver it — contact details, business details, and any system access you explicitly grant — is processed for that purpose only. Systems built for you are yours. Any credentials you provide are held only as long as the work requires and are handed back or destroyed on request.

Children. This site and these services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 16. No information is knowingly collected from children.

Your rights

Because there are no accounts and no tracking, there is usually nothing held about you at all. Where there is — an email you sent, or records from paid work — you may ask for a copy, a correction, or its deletion, and you may object to its use. Write to russ@blvkware.dev and it will be handled within 30 days at no cost.

Depending on where you live, these rights may be guaranteed to you by law, including the UK and EU GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act. BlvkWare does not sell personal information, under any definition, to anyone.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because there is no server, no database and no account system, there is no store of user data to breach. The main security consideration is your own AI provider key, which is why it stays on your device and is never transmitted to BlvkWare.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be described plainly rather than buried.
