Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

BlvkWare does not run analytics, does not set cookies of its own, and has no user accounts. The free tools on this site run entirely inside your browser. Some things do use a server: the kit service, the HALLUX and CORDON APIs, and the payments for them. This page explains precisely what each part does with your information, who else receives it, and how long it is kept.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is William Wheeler, trading as BlvkWare, P.O. Box 406, Wilmer, AL 36587, United States. BlvkWare is a sole operation and sells to businesses. For anything on this page, including any privacy request, write to russ@blvkware.dev or to that postal address.

What this website collects

No accounts. No cookies of our own. No analytics. No advertising or tracking pixels. There is no sign-up anywhere on this site and no profile is ever built about you.

Hosting logs

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages, through Cloudflare, which also answers the site's agent endpoints (such as /api, /a2a and /ucp). Like any web host, both record standard request data (your IP address, browser user agent, and which address was requested) for delivery, security and abuse prevention, under their own privacy statements. BlvkWare does not keep these logs.

Fonts

Typefaces are served from blvkware.dev itself. They are open-licensed fonts (Manrope, Fira Code and DM Mono) hosted alongside the pages, so reading the site does not make your browser contact Google Fonts or any other font service.

What the tools do with your input

Business Scan, Agent Designer and App Builder are client-side applications. Their code runs on your device. There is no BlvkWare backend receiving your prompts, your API key, or your results.

Your AI provider key

The tools need an AI model to work. On this public site you supply your own key from a provider you choose. That key is stored in your browser's localStorage, on your own device, and is sent directly from your browser to the provider you selected (Google AI Studio, OpenRouter, or Groq) over HTTPS. It never passes through BlvkWare. Clearing your browser storage, or using the "Forget key" button, removes it permanently.

Whatever you type into a tool is sent to that provider along with the request, and is handled under their privacy terms. Read those if you intend to enter anything sensitive.

Data kept in your browser

The tools store a small amount of state in localStorage on your device: your chosen provider and API key, your light/dark theme preference, in App Builder the builds you've generated, and on the kit page the agent design you are working on, so they survive a page reload. None of it leaves your device except as described under the kit service below. Clearing site data in your browser deletes all of it.

Business Scan and third-party page readers

Business Scan analyses a public web page you nominate. A browser cannot fetch an arbitrary website directly, so the address you enter is passed to public read-only relay services, currently corsproxy.io, api.allorigins.win and r.jina.ai, which retrieve the page and return it. Those services see the URL you asked for and your IP address. They are independent third parties operating under their own terms. Only the address is sent; no other information about you is included.

Downloading a report or a design

The download buttons in Business Scan and Agent Designer build the file in your browser and save it to your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Designing and buying a kit

The kit service

The kit page sends the design you make to BlvkWare's kit service: the role, the systems, channels and capabilities you picked, the autonomy level, and your answers about your business, including any rules you write. The service uses it to list the files in your kit and, when you download, to generate the kit. The design is not stored. Once the response is sent it is gone, and BlvkWare keeps no copy of it or of your kit.

The kit service runs on Netlify, which records standard request data (your IP address, browser user agent and the time) as any host does, under Netlify's own privacy policy.

Your licence key

When you download, the kit service checks your licence key with Gumroad. It keeps one record per key: a one-way fingerprint of the key, which cannot be turned back into the key, with the role it unlocked, the kit size, the times of its first and latest download, and a download count. That is all. No name, no email address and no design is kept with it. The key itself is not stored in your browser either.

Gumroad

Kits are bought on Gumroad, which collects what it needs to take your payment and issue your key, under Gumroad's own privacy policy. BlvkWare never sees your card details. As the seller, BlvkWare can see the purchase in its Gumroad account, including the email address you bought with. That is used only for your purchase, to answer you or to handle a refund, and is never added to a marketing list.

The HALLUX and CORDON APIs

HALLUX (api.blvkware.dev) and CORDON (cordon.blvkware.dev) run on Cloudflare and keep their records in Cloudflare's database service.

  • Free-tier quota: to count the free daily allowance, the API stores the calling IP address with a daily count. These rows are deleted automatically after 3 days. Daily statistics about calls turned away for payment keep only a truncated one-way hash of the address, never the address.
  • API keys: a key is shown to you once and never stored. Only a one-way fingerprint is kept, with its plan, credit balance, status and the dates it was created and last changed.
  • Paid keys: the email address you give at checkout is kept with the key so we can send your receipt and confirmation and let you recover a lost key. For a subscription we also keep Paddle's customer and subscription IDs, so Paddle's notifications can switch the key on, renew it or switch it off. For a credit pack we keep the PayPal order ID.
  • What you send for checking: the package names, model names and page addresses you submit are used to answer the request. Public facts about those packages are cached, without anything identifying you, so later checks are fast.
  • Per-call payments (x402): we keep the transaction ID, the paying wallet address and the amount. These are already public on the blockchain.
  • Agent checkout: if an AI agent starts a purchase for you at blvkware.dev/ucp, the session (the item and any name or email the agent passes) is held for six hours, or 30 days once paid, and then deleted.

Payments

BlvkWare never sees or stores your card or bank details. Each payment provider collects what it needs to take the payment, under its own privacy policy:

  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited and its affiliates) sells HALLUX Team and Feed subscriptions as our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle receives your name, email, billing address and payment details, handles tax and invoicing, and shares with us your email, country and the details of your order and subscription. See Paddle's privacy notice.
  • PayPal takes payment for prepaid credit packs. We receive the order ID, the amount and, if you give it, your email.
  • Gumroad takes payment for agent kits, as described above.

Email we send

Receipts, key confirmations and key-recovery links for paid API keys are sent through ZeptoMail (Zoho). They contain your key's prefix, never the full key. They are transactional only: no newsletters, no marketing.

When you email BlvkWare

Email sent to russ@blvkware.dev is received and stored in Zoho Mail. Your address and the content of your message are kept so the conversation can continue. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never added to a marketing list without you asking. Ask and it will be deleted.

Children. This site and its kits are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 16. No information is knowingly collected from children.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide what you asked for (performing our contract with you): answering API calls, issuing and running your key, delivering kits, sending receipts and recovery links.
  • Legitimate interests: preventing abuse and fraud, enforcing the free allowance, keeping the services secure, and answering email you send.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records that tax and accounting law requires.

Who receives it

Only the service providers named on this page, each for the purpose described: Cloudflare, GitHub, Netlify, Paddle (as Merchant of Record), PayPal, Gumroad, Zoho (Mail and ZeptoMail), and, for the free tools, the AI provider you choose and the page-reading relays. Beyond those, information is disclosed only to professional advisers such as an accountant or lawyer where needed, or to authorities where the law requires it. It is never sold.

How long it is kept

  • Free-tier IP addresses: 3 days.
  • Agent checkout sessions: six hours, or 30 days once paid.
  • Key fingerprints and plan records: while the key exists, then deleted on request.
  • Email addresses and order records for paid keys: while the key exists and for up to 7 years after the last purchase, where tax and accounting law requires purchase records to be kept. Anything not needed for that is deleted on request.
  • Email correspondence: until you ask for it to be deleted, or when it is no longer needed.

International transfers

BlvkWare and most of its providers are in the United States, so your information is processed there. Where you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers use, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies

BlvkWare sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The checkout pages load Paddle's or PayPal's payment form, which may set cookies that are strictly necessary to take the payment securely and prevent fraud, under that provider's own cookie policy. The free tools keep their settings in your browser's localStorage, as described above.

Your rights

Because there are no accounts and no tracking, there is usually little or nothing held about you. Where there is (an email you sent, a purchase, or a paid key), you may ask for access to it, a copy in a portable form, correction, deletion, or restriction of its use, and you may object to its use or withdraw any consent you gave. Write to russ@blvkware.dev and it will be answered within one month, at no cost. If you are unhappy with the answer, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Depending on where you live, these rights may be guaranteed to you by law, including the UK and EU GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act. BlvkWare does not sell personal information, under any definition, to anyone.

Security

Everything is served over HTTPS. API keys, recovery links and licence keys are stored only as one-way fingerprints that cannot be turned back into keys, card details never reach BlvkWare, and access to the records that do exist is limited to the person who runs the business. The kit service keeps no design and no kit. Your own AI provider key stays on your device and is never transmitted to BlvkWare.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be described plainly rather than buried.
