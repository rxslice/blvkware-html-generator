## First, an honest word about backlinks

Links from Facebook and X are `nofollow`. They pass no ranking signal directly,
so anybody telling you to post for "backlink juice" is describing something that
stopped working around 2010. What they actually do is worth having anyway:

1. **Discovery.** A crawler that sees the URL somewhere active fetches it sooner.
2. **Entity consolidation.** Both profiles are now in the site's `sameAs`, so
   Google and the answer engines can tell the same organisation is behind the
   site, the Facebook page and the X account. That is a real signal, and it is
   already shipped.
3. **Humans.** Some of them click, and a few of those link from somewhere that
   does count.

The links that will move this page are editorial ones, and this page has an
unusually good hook for earning them: **it corrects a statistic the whole
category gets wrong.** That is the pitch, not "we wrote a blog post."

## Where an editorial link could actually come from

Ordered by how likely they are to say yes.

- **r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur.** Post the calculator, not the article, and
  lead with the fact that it argues against buying. Read each subreddit's
  self-promotion rule first; several require you to be a participant already.
- **Hacker News.** The MIT correction is the submission, titled as the
  correction. The calculator is what keeps people on the page. Expect the
  comments to check your arithmetic, which is fine, because it checks out.
- **Anybody who cited the statistic wrongly.** Search for the phrase, email the
  author, point at the primary source, mention your page carries the correction.
  This is slow, it works, and most people are glad to be told.
- **Local business associations and chambers.** The Mississippi angle plus a free
  tool is an easy newsletter item.
- **Accountants and bookkeepers.** They are asked "should we automate this?"
  constantly and have nothing good to hand over. A calculator that sometimes says
  no is exactly what a professional adviser is willing to forward.
